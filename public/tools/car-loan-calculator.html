Calculate your monthly car payment under Malaysia's new hire-purchase rules.

What changed in 2026

The Hire-Purchase (Amendment) Act 2026 took effect on 1 June 2026, replacing the old flat-rate + Rule of 78 method with the effective interest rate (EIR) / reducing-balance method. New loans use reducing balance; loans taken before 1 June 2026 may still use the old flat-rate method. Use the toggle below to compare.
