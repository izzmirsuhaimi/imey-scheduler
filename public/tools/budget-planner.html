The 50/30/20 rule

  • 50% Needs — rent, groceries, utilities, transport, minimum debt payments.
  • 30% Wants — dining out, hobbies, subscriptions, entertainment.
  • 20% Savings — emergency fund, investments, extra debt repayment.

Adjust the percentages to fit your situation — the tool recalculates instantly.
