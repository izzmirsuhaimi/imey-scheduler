How EPF works

EPF is Malaysia's mandatory retirement savings scheme. Employees contribute 11% of salary; employers contribute 13% (salary ≤ RM5,000) or 12% (salary > RM5,000). Dividends are declared annually (recent: 6.15% for 2025). Since May 2024, 10% of contributions flow to the flexible Akaun Fleksibel (Account 3). Retirement withdrawal age is 55 (full) / 60.
