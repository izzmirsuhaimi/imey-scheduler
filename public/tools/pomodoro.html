Pomodoro Timer

25-minute focus sessions with 5-minute breaks. Stay disciplined, one session at a time.

How to use

  1. Pick a task and start a 25-minute focus session.
  2. Work without interruption until the timer ends.
  3. Take a 5-minute break.
  4. After 4 sessions, take a longer 15–30 minute break.
