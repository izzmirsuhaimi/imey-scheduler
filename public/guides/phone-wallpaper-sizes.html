Phone wallpaper sizes: iPhone & Android cheat sheet

Use these dimensions when sizing a timetable wallpaper. The Scheduler-inator! sets them automatically when you pick a device.

iPhone

  • iPhone 15 / 15 Pro — 1179 × 2556
  • iPhone 15 Pro Max — 1290 × 2796
  • iPhone 14 / 13 / 12 — 1170 × 2532
  • iPhone 14 Pro Max / 13 Pro Max — 1284 × 2778
  • iPhone SE (3rd gen) — 750 × 1334
  • iPhone 11 / XR — 828 × 1792

Android (common)

  • Samsung Galaxy S24 / S23 — 1080 × 2340
  • Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra — 1440 × 3120
  • Google Pixel 8 / 7 — 1080 × 2400
  • Google Pixel 8 Pro — 1344 × 2992
  • OnePlus 12 — 1440 × 3168
  • Xiaomi 14 — 1200 × 2670

Tablets & desktops

  • iPad (10th gen) — 1640 × 2360
  • iPad Pro 12.9" — 2048 × 2732
  • MacBook Air 13" — 2560 × 1664
  • Full HD monitor — 1920 × 1080
  • 4K monitor — 3840 × 2160

Tips for a perfect fit

  • Match the aspect ratio, not just the pixels — a 9:19.5 design on a 9:20 screen will crop.
  • Leave a safe zone for notches, dynamic islands, and home indicators.
  • Export at the device's native resolution for the sharpest result.
  • When in doubt, pick the device in The Scheduler-inator! and let it size the canvas.
