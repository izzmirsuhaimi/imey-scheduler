Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

The Scheduler-inator! ("we", "us", "our") is a free personal organization and productivity toolbox available at scheduler-inator.site. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and tools.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use The Scheduler-inator!. The tools on this site (timetable maker, calculators, and other utilities) run entirely in your browser. Any data you enter — such as class schedules, background images, or calculator inputs — is stored locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage. We do not transmit, store, or have access to that data on our servers.

2. Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or optout.networkadvertising.org.

3. Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand aggregate usage (for example, which pages are popular). These tools do not collect personally identifiable information and are used only to improve the site.

4. Third-party links

Our guides and pages may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

5. Children's privacy

The Scheduler-inator! is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data security

Because your data stays on your own device, its security depends on your device and browser. We recommend keeping your browser and operating system up to date.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
