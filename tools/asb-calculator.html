What is ASB?

Amanah Saham Bumiputera (ASB) is a unit trust fund managed by Permodalan Nasional Berhad (PNB), primarily for Bumiputera Malaysians. Units are priced at a fixed RM1.00, with no sales charge. Dividends are declared annually (typically in December/January) and are usually reinvested, so your money compounds. ASB is considered low-risk and government-linked. ASB financing is also offered by most banks.

Fact check: The individual investment cap was raised from RM200,000 to RM300,000 (effective 2023). Dividends are based on the lowest balance in each month, so this calculator uses a simplified monthly-compounding estimate.
