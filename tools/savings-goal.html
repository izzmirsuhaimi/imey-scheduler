How to use this

  1. Set a specific, measurable target (e.g. "emergency fund of $5,000").
  2. Enter what you already have saved.
  3. Pick a realistic deadline in months.
  4. The calculator shows the monthly amount needed, assuming a steady interest rate.

Tips for hitting your goal

  • Automate it. Set a recurring transfer on payday so the money moves before you can spend it.
  • Start small. A small consistent amount beats an ambitious plan you abandon.
  • Revisit monthly. If income changes, adjust the contribution, not the goal.
  • Separate accounts. Keep savings out of your spending account.
