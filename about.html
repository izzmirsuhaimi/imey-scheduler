About The Scheduler-inator!

The Scheduler-inator! is a free, browser-based personal organization and productivity toolbox created by izzmirsuhaimi. It started as a simple idea: turn a class schedule into a beautiful wallpaper sized perfectly for your phone, tablet, or monitor — no sign-up, no watermark, no cost.

Since then it has grown into a small toolkit for anyone who wants a more organized, disciplined, and intentional life: timetable and schedule wallpapers, study and productivity guides, and personal finance calculators that help you plan savings, investments, and budgets.

Our philosophy

  • Free and accessible. Every tool runs in your browser. No account, no paywall, no data leaving your device.
  • Private by default. Your schedules, images, and calculator inputs stay on your own device via localStorage.
  • Practical over flashy. We focus on tools and guides that you will actually use to organize your day and your money.
  • Honest and transparent. Calculators are illustrative, not financial advice. Guides are written to be useful, not clickbait.

What's inside

  • Timetable Wallpaper Maker — design a class schedule wallpaper for any screen ratio and download it instantly.
  • Guides — practical articles on study schedules, time management, and personal organization.
  • Finance Tools — compound interest, savings goals, and budget calculators to support long-term discipline.

Who it's for

Students, teachers, and anyone building better habits — whether you are organizing a new semester, planning a study routine, or taking the first steps toward financial discipline. The Scheduler-inator! is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Disney or Phineas and Ferb.
